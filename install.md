## **HOW TO INSTALL:**

**STEP 1:**
OPEN STEAM

**STEP 2:**
GO TO `The Jackbox Party Pack 5` IN YOUR LIBRARY

**STEP 3:**
CLICK THE GEAR ICON, `Manage`, AND THEN `Browse Local Files`

**STEP 4:**
OPEN `games` THEN `RapBattle`

**STEP 5:**
CREATE A NEW FOLDER CALLED `backup` AND COPY THE ENTIRE `RapBattle` FOLDER INTO IT

**STEP 6:** 
DRAG THE CONTENTS OF THE .zip FOLDER INTO THE `RapBattle` FOLDER. CLICKING REPLACE IF PROMPTED

**STEP 7:**
CONGRATULATIONS YOU HAVE SUCCESSFULLY INSTALLED THE MOD 

## TO UNINSTALL

DRAG THE CONTENTS OF THE `backup` FOLDER BACK INTO THE `RapBattle` FOLDER.

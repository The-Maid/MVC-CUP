## PRESENTING: ***THE MAID'S CLEANED UP PROMPTS: MAD VERSE CITY***

Are you tired of endlessly vague prompts, in your Party Pack 5 Game. Well fret no more!
With the maid's cleaned up prompts 87%* of that vagueness has been removed!

**WHAT DOES IT DO?**

This mod works to add more context to prompts that needed it to make your bars less janky, sometimes making the final line more clear, sometimes as simple as making it clear that the adjective should be positive or negative. The final sentences being read aloud remain unchanged, it is only the prompt you are sent.

> Example of a prompt that is changed
> 
> Text: `"I never fall down I stand tall like the <blank>"`
> 
> Original Prompt: `"proper noun"`
> 
> New Prompt:`"proper noun - famous building"`

**HOW DOES IT WORK?**

It works by replacing the list of prompts that Jackbox uses with a different list of far less vague ones. This also means that this mod only needs to be installed by the host, and will work through twitch streams without the audience having to do anything different.
